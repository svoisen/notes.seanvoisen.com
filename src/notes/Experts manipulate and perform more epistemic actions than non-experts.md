[[Epistemic actions are taken to think, not to progress toward a goal]]. In their paper, Kirsh and Maglio use an extended example of Tetris to show that experts tend to perform more epistemic actions than non-experts. Advanced Tetris players, for instance, will manipulate a piece in space as it falls to see where it should fit, rather than try to mentally envision the various rotations in order to figure out the right location. It is proven to be faster to think via direct manipulation than to think without such manipulation.
## References

Kirsh, D. and Maglio, P. (1994) On Distinguishing Epistemic from Pragmatic Action.
