Hello! I'm [Sean Voisen](https://seanvoisen.com). Welcome to my public notebook.

You can think of this place like a kind of [digital garden](https://nesslabs.com/digital-garden-set-up). These pages contain my evergreen notes: short, linked ideas about design, cognition, philosophy, software and—occasionally—leadership. Each note may change and evolve with my own thinking. 

Publishing these notes is an experiment in developing a stronger practice for "thinking in the open" without the self-imposed editorial constraints that come with writing an essay or blog post.

If you're looking for a place to get started, you can take a look at what's currently [top of mind](/top-of-mind) in my thinking, reading and research.
